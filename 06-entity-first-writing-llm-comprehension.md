# Entity-First Writing: Structuring Content for LLM Comprehension

**Meta description:** Semantic SEO in 2026 means writing for entities, not just keywords. A practical guide to restructuring content so AI systems parse and cite it correctly.

**Focus keyword:** entity-first content / semantic SEO

---

Keyword density stopped being the deciding ranking factor years ago. What's replaced it — and what generative AI models rely on even more heavily — is **entity recognition**: whether a piece of content clearly identifies distinct, well-defined concepts (people, places, products, organizations) and the relationships between them.

## What Is an Entity, Practically Speaking?

An entity is anything a search engine or language model can treat as a discrete "thing" with attributes: your company, your product line, a competitor, a certification, a location. Entity-first writing makes those things explicit rather than implied.

## Five Rules for Entity-First Content

1. **Name things precisely and consistently.** Don't alternate between "our platform," "the software," and "the tool" — pick one name and use it throughout.
2. **Define the entity early.** The first mention of your product or service should include a one-sentence definition, even if it feels repetitive to a human reader who already knows what you do.
3. **State relationships explicitly.** "Our platform integrates with Salesforce" is entity-clear. "It works well with your existing CRM" is vague and harder for a model to map.
4. **Use structured data to reinforce entities.** Schema.org markup (Organization, Product, Person) gives machines a second, unambiguous confirmation of what you've written in prose.
5. **Link entities to authoritative sources.** Referencing recognized standards, certifications, or well-known companies helps models place your brand within a known knowledge graph.

## Why This Matters for AI Citation

When ChatGPT or Perplexity generates an answer, it's assembling fragments from sources it trusts to correctly represent named entities. Vague, pronoun-heavy writing is harder to extract cleanly and gets passed over in favor of a competitor's more explicit content — even if your product is objectively better.

## The Takeaway

Rewriting your top pages with explicit entity naming and clear relationships is one of the highest-leverage content changes you can make in 2026 — it helps human skimmers and AI extractors at the same time.
