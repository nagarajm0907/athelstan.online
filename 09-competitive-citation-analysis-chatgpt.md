# Competitive Citation Analysis: Who Is ChatGPT Actually Recommending?

**Meta description:** A repeatable method for auditing which brands generative engines currently cite in your category — and the content gaps you can exploit to take that spot.

**Focus keyword:** competitive citation analysis / AI search competitor research

---

Traditional competitor research checks who ranks on page one of Google. In 2026, that's only half the picture. The other half is finding out who ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews are actually naming when someone asks about your category — because that citation happens whether or not the user ever visits a website.

## Step 1: Build a Question Bank

List 15–20 real questions your target customer would ask an AI assistant while researching your category — comparisons, "best of" questions, and problem-first questions ("how do I fix X").

## Step 2: Run the Questions Across Engines

Ask each question in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google (checking for an AI Overview) using a logged-out or incognito session to avoid personalization skewing results. Record which brands get named, and in what context — recommended, mentioned as an option, or cited as a source.

## Step 3: Identify the Pattern

Look for what the consistently cited competitors have in common: do they have detailed comparison pages? Strong review presence? Frequently updated content? This tells you what the models are pattern-matching on, not just who happens to win.

## Step 4: Find the Content Gap

Cross-reference the questions where your brand didn't appear against your existing content. Usually there's a specific missing page type — a comparison page, a pricing breakdown, a use-case guide — rather than a wholesale content problem.

## Step 5: Re-Test After Publishing

AI search indexes update faster than traditional SEO in some cases. Re-run your question bank four to six weeks after publishing new content to see whether citation patterns shifted.

## The Takeaway

You can't manage what you don't measure. Running a structured citation audit quarterly turns "we should probably show up more in AI search" into a specific, trackable content roadmap.
