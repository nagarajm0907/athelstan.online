# Local SEO in the AI Era: Structured Data for Map & Voice Search

**Meta description:** Local pack rankings and AI voice assistants both lean on structured business data. A checklist for getting your NAP, schema, and reviews AI-ready.

**Focus keyword:** local SEO structured data

---

"Find me a plumber near me" used to return a Google Maps pack. Now it might return a spoken answer from a voice assistant, or a single recommendation inside an AI chat response. Local SEO hasn't gotten less important — it's gotten more dependent on clean, structured data.

## The Foundation: NAP Consistency

Name, Address, and Phone number must match exactly across your website, Google Business Profile, and every directory listing. Even small discrepancies (Street vs St., a missing suite number) reduce confidence signals for both traditional local pack rankings and AI assistants pulling business details.

## Structured Data That Matters Most

- **LocalBusiness schema** with precise address, service area, hours, and price range
- **Review schema** reflecting your actual aggregate rating, kept current
- **Service schema** for each distinct offering, rather than one generic "services" block
- **FAQPage schema** answering the specific questions local customers ask (pricing, service area, turnaround time)

## Optimizing for Voice and Conversational Queries

Voice and AI assistant queries tend to be longer and more conversational than typed searches — "who's the best-rated electrician near downtown open right now" instead of "electrician downtown." Structure your content and FAQ schema to answer these full, natural-language questions directly rather than only targeting short keyword phrases.

## Don't Neglect Your Google Business Profile

It remains the single highest-weighted local signal. Keep hours accurate, respond to every review, post updates regularly, and upload real photos — all of it feeds directly into how confidently both Google and AI systems recommend you.

## The Takeaway

Local SEO in 2026 is a data-hygiene discipline as much as a content one. Get your structured data and cross-platform consistency right, and you become the obvious, verifiable answer for both a map pin and a spoken recommendation.
