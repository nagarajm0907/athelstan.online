What Gets Scored

Four signals that decide if AI engines trust your site

Your score isn't a vague grade. It's built from the same signals AI chatbots and AI search actually weigh when deciding what to cite.

01 / CRAWLABILITY

Can AI crawlers reach your content?

Robots.txt rules, JavaScript-rendered content, sitemap coverage, and response times, the basics that determine whether a crawler can even see your pages.

02 / STRUCTURED DATA

Is your business machine-readable?

Organization, Service, FAQ and Product schema coverage, the structured confirmation AI systems use to verify what your prose claims.

03 / ANSWER-READINESS

Are your pages built to be extracted?

Whether key pages lead with a direct answer, use clear headings, and structure content in a way models can confidently quote and attribute.

04 / ENTITY CLARITY

Do you state who you are, plainly?

Consistent naming, clear definitions, and unambiguous descriptions of your product or service, the difference between being findable and being guessed at.
