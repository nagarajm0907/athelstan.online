# Answer Engine Optimization: Winning the Zero-Click SERP

**Meta description:** AEO is the discipline behind featured snippets and AI Overview citations. Here's the exact content structure that earns the box.

**Focus keyword:** Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

---

More than half of Google searches now end without a click. The searcher gets their answer directly in a featured snippet, People Also Ask box, or AI Overview — and moves on. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of making sure your content is the source that answer comes from.

## How Answer Boxes Choose a Source

Google and AI Overview systems favor content that answers a specific question in a self-contained, extractable block — typically 40 to 60 words, positioned near a matching heading, with no unnecessary preamble.

## The AEO Content Structure

1. **Use the question as the heading.** Match the exact phrasing a user would type or ask, as an H2 or H3.
2. **Answer in the first sentence.** Don't build up to it — state the answer, then support it in the following sentences.
3. **Use lists and tables for multi-part answers.** Step-by-step processes and comparisons extract cleanly into rich snippets.
4. **Add FAQ schema.** Structured FAQ markup gives search engines a machine-readable version of your Q&A content, increasing the chance it's surfaced directly.
5. **Keep answers current.** Update statistics and dates yearly — stale answers get replaced by fresher competing content.

## AEO vs Traditional SEO

Traditional SEO optimizes to earn a click. AEO optimizes to earn the citation even without a click — which means the value shifts from traffic volume to brand visibility and trust. A business cited repeatedly in AI Overviews builds authority even on searches that never land a visitor on the site.

## The Takeaway

You can't opt out of zero-click search, but you can decide whether your brand is the one being cited in it. Restructure your highest-intent pages around direct, extractable answers, and track which of them start appearing in the answer box.
